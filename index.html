About the farm

A working farm, run the modern way.

Nai'Posha started as a small dairy herd and has grown into a full agricultural operation — dairy cattle, poultry, pigs, sheep, goats, a tree seedling nursery, and crop fields, backed by a farm inputs supply arm and a consultancy service that helps neighbouring farmers improve their own yields. Every animal leaving our gate is health-checked, and every visitor is welcome to see how we work.

Livestock

Dairy cattle bred for consistent, healthy milk.

Our dairy herd is a mix of Friesian and Ayrshire cattle, chosen for strong milk yield and good health in our local climate, on a strict feeding, vaccination, and milking hygiene routine.

  • Friesian & Ayrshire dairy breeds
  • Regular veterinary health checks
  • Clean, hygienic milking routine
  • Pasture rotation for better feed

Poultry

Layers and broilers, raised with room to move.

Our poultry unit keeps layers for a steady daily supply of eggs, and broilers raised for meat, in well-ventilated housing on a balanced, stage-specific feeding plan.

  • Free-range layer flock
  • Broilers raised to market weight
  • Balanced, stage-specific feeding
  • Routine disease control programme
